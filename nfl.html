What am I looking at
Not recommendations. Rank order is a hypothesis about which rows to grade first, until outcomes.py has graded a full week.

Raw Mkt P is a de-vigged, sharpness-weighted consensus of books posting the exact same line as DGF (pure market, no model input). Norm Mkt P is the same consensus plus books at other lines re-expressed at the DGF line (Poisson for count stats ≤10, Normal with the model's σ otherwise) — this is the primary ranking, and it is not model-free.

Model P is our own model's probability for the same side, built independently from the projection and its uncertainty — it never looks at the market. Model Edge is Model P − Mkt P (Norm) in percentage points: positive means our model likes this side more than the market does; negative means the market likes it more than our model does. This is a probability comparison, not a priced wager — a small Model Edge and a large one can both say "Both Agree," which is why the Signal chip now shows the pp gap alongside the label instead of just the bare label.

Book spread shows the de-vigged fair probability of this side across contributing books — low to high, i.e. how much the books disagree with each other. Where a feed gives us both sides (Pinnacle and the Action Network books do), the expanded row's price ladder shows the Over and the Under price at each book, and the fair % is a true two-way de-vig rather than an assumed hold.

vs breakeven (next to Mkt P) is Norm Mkt P minus this anchor's own loosest real pick'em breakeven — PrizePicks and Underdog each have their own number (~54.7% / ~53.5%, from their actual payout tables), not a shared placeholder. DraftKings Pick 3 shows no number here: it's peer-to-peer with prize-pool-floor payouts, not a fixed multiplier, so there's no static breakeven to compare against.

Bet check is the same comparison but for Model P instead of the market's: does our own model clear this platform's loosest breakeven by more than the margin entry_builder.py requires before it will use a leg? BET clears it with room, THIN clears it but inside the margin, PASS doesn't clear it, N/A means no static breakeven exists (DK Pick 3) or there's no model probability to check. This is still not a full entry's priced EV — that depends on the actual entry size and any leg correlation — it's "does this leg have a real chance of being worth including at all."

Rows priced by fewer than 2 books aren't ranked. Availability-unresolved players are held out of the main list below.

Matchup (in a row's expanded detail) answers one question: how often do players at this position beat their own projected number against this defense — not what the whole position group racked up, which is what this panel used to show. The reference is the model's walk-forward projection, because real posted-line history only starts 2026-09-08. Measured on the stored residuals, 2022-2025: the lean repeats for RB rushing (split-half r = +0.26 attempts, +0.33 yards, both 95% intervals clearing zero) and does not for WR receptions (+0.04) or QB pass attempts (+0.14). Every row prints its own stability figure, so a number that does not carry week to week says so on its face. Note the league baseline is about 40%, not 50% — these stats are right-skewed, so going under a mean projection is the normal case and only the gap from league means anything. The DFS section below still shows position-group context for DK Classic players, informational only, never folded into the fantasy-point projection.

Distribution (in a row's expanded detail) shows the line against the model's μ ± 1σ band and the actual P(Over)/P(Under) split — a big projection-vs-line gap can still carry a middling win probability when σ is large, and the raw projection number alone doesn't show that.
